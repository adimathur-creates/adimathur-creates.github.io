Under Seven Days

Rupeeflo promises an NRI demat account live in under 7 days, across four systems it doesn't fully own: the app, the notary call, the DHL pickup and Zerodha. This is the analysis I would do in my first weeks: what to measure, where the data disagrees with itself, where applications really stall, and whether WhatsApp, email and push nudges recover anyone.

Aditya Mathur · October 2026 · prepared for Rupeeflo's product team

Read this first. I have no Rupeeflo data. The journey (6 documents, about a day of checks, a 15-minute video notary, DHL pickup, about 5 days at Zerodha) and the rework causes come from Rupeeflo's public product page. The 9,000 applications, rates and timings are synthetic and my assumptions. Everything on this page, including the SQL console, runs on that synthetic data in your browser.

    2 · What to measure

    One number to steer by, and what moves it

    North star

    % of applications live within 7 days of documents

    Matches the promise customers see. Measured from the ops database, not product events.

    Inputs

    Stage conversion, stage time, rework rate

    Rework split by reason: route, document type, missed notary call, signature mismatch. Each has a different owner.

    Guardrails

    Wrong nudges, support tickets, cost per live account

    A nudge sent to someone who already finished a step costs trust. Counted, not ignored.

    9 · First 30 days
    Week 1Map every event to its source system. Build the ops-database funnel and compare it with Mixpanel or Amplitude, stage by stage.
    Week 2Reason codes for every rejection. Rework dashboard by country, route and reason, shared with ops daily.
    Week 3Holdout on every CRM journey. Switch nudge triggers from product events to ops status. Suppress courier and broker-stage nudges.
    Week 4First readout: what moved the 7-day rate, what did not, and the next two experiments.
    Limits
    • All volumes, rates, timings and nudge effects are assumptions. The structure of the analysis is the point; the real numbers come from Rupeeflo's data.
    • The notary hours (10:00–19:00 IST) are my assumption, not a published fact.
    • Several results vary between reruns. I show ranges where a single run would mislead.
