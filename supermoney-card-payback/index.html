Card Payback

super.money is turning free UPI into a business through FD-backed secured credit cards. This page works out what one card earns and costs in its first year, where the funnel leaks, and three product changes that turn a loss-making card into a profitable one.

Aditya Mathur · October 2026 · prepared for the super.money product team

Read this first. I do not work at super.money and have no internal data. The model runs 100,000 synthetic users through the secured-card funnel and 12 months of use. Funnel rates, fees, revenue shares and spending patterns are my assumptions, and every one is listed. Public facts are linked at the end. Treat the rupee figures as directions, not forecasts. With real funnel and transaction data, the same model rebuilds in a week.
1 · THE PROBLEM

Most of the card's spend earns nothing

  • UPI is free and costs money. 5% cashback is the hook. The company has said it costs ₹10–20 per user per month.
  • Revenue comes from cross-sell. Per TechCrunch (October 2025): about 80% personal loans, 10% cards and 10% payment products. The Kotak811 partnership aims for about 200,000 secured cards a month, about 60% to first-time borrowers.
  • The catch. RuPay credit cards on UPI earn zero interchange on payments up to ₹2,000 at small merchants. 85% of super.money transactions are scan-and-pay, and the average RuPay-on-UPI ticket is about ₹850. So most card spend earns nothing while cashback is still paid on it. Axis capped the super.money card's 3% cashback from January 2026, which points to the same pressure.

Which users should get the secured-card offer, how should the card be used at the scanner, and what has to happen after issue, so that a card pays for itself in its first 12 months?

In this model, a card breaks even today only if the bank pays about ₹480 per activated card. With the three fixes, about ₹255 is enough. That is a stronger position in any partner negotiation, and it means new bank partners can be signed on thinner terms.

7 · RECOMMENDATION

What I would ship, in order

  1. Smart pay at the scanner

    The biggest single fix. Cashback per active card falls from about ₹14 to about ₹4 a month, and it mirrors what Axis already did by capping cashback. It is a product change, not a contract change, so we control it.

  2. One-tap FD funding

    The biggest leak is between KYC done and FD funded: KYC has been paid for, and no card comes out. One tap with a suggested amount converts more of them and lifts limits.

  3. Credit-builder path to loans

    Loans are 80% of revenue. A visible streak, autopay and a pre-approved offer at month 6 roughly triple loans from each card cohort in the model.

  4. Do not narrow the offer to high spenders

    It sounds right but loses money per card in the model. At these limits, interchange is small for everyone. What pays is activation and the loan path, not spend size.

8 · SPEC FOR SMART PAY

Feature spec

User story

As a superCard holder scanning a QR, I want the app to suggest the right way to pay, so that I earn cashback where it is offered, keep my limit for bigger buys, and build my credit score.

Scope

Scan-and-pay and in-app checkout for users with an active secured card. Not P2P transfers.

P0Classify each payment at scan: merchant type from the QR (small or large merchant), amount, online or offline.
P0Preselect the card where the payment earns interchange, and the bank account elsewhere. One tap to switch. Remember the choice per merchant.
P0Card cashback rules updated to earning payments only, with plain-language terms shown before the first payment.
P1Monthly card summary: cashback earned, limit used, repayment streak and score progress.
Metrics
  • Main: card contribution per active card per month.
  • Secondary: share of card spend that earns interchange.
  • Guardrails: month-3 card activity, overrides of the suggestion, cashback complaints, and 30-day UPI retention.
Test design
  • Randomise by user, 50/50, among active card holders.
  • Run 4 weeks, so every user passes a full statement cycle.
  • Ship if contribution rises and month-3 activity does not fall by more than 2 points.
Funnel query (stage conversion by weekly cohort)
SELECT DATE_TRUNC('week', offer_seen_at)               AS cohort_week,
       COUNT(*)                                         AS offered,
       COUNT(started_at)        * 1.0 / COUNT(*)        AS start_rate,
       COUNT(kyc_done_at)       * 1.0 / NULLIF(COUNT(started_at), 0)  AS kyc_rate,
       COUNT(fd_funded_at)      * 1.0 / NULLIF(COUNT(kyc_done_at), 0) AS fd_rate,
       COUNT(upi_linked_at)     * 1.0 / NULLIF(COUNT(fd_funded_at), 0) AS link_rate,
       COUNT(CASE WHEN first_credit_txn_at <= upi_linked_at + INTERVAL '30 days'
                  THEN 1 END)   * 1.0 / NULLIF(COUNT(upi_linked_at), 0) AS first_spend_rate,
       AVG(fd_amount)                                   AS avg_fd
FROM   secured_card_funnel
GROUP  BY 1
ORDER  BY 1;

10 · LIMITS

What this model does not know

  • Every rate is an assumption: funnel rates, FD sizes, the bank's acquisition fee, super.money's share of interchange, cashback funding and loan commissions. The direction held in every stress test; the sizes will not.
  • The interchange rule for RuPay credit on UPI can change. A change at small merchants would shift the whole picture, so the classifier should read the rule from config, not code.
  • Revolving interest, card fees and the effect on the core 5% UPI cashback are left out.
